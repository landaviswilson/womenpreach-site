Take It Home

Assimilation, Accommodation, & Mutuality Exercise
A free tool for church leaders to ask one honest question: when someone different joins us, do we expect them to become like us, make a little room for them, or let ourselves be changed? Twenty-five short stories, including the first woman pastor, let a council, staff, or small group name where their church's center really sits, and a ten-question self-check turns it into next steps. It gives church leadership a shared language for talking about cultural competency, and about how a congregation receives a clergywoman, without anyone having to be the one to raise it.
